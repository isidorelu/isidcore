Isidcore is a creative studio and blog founded by Isidore Lu (isid or). The studio produces music, film, and live events where pop, Japanese culture, and technology meet. The blog publishes short writing on culture, arts, and technology.

Isidcore began as a blog on Substack and Instagram. In summer 2026 it became a production company. Its first project was a self-financed $10K campaign: a full-production music video, a Japanese mini-album, a merchandise line, and two release parties in New York. That same summer the studio took on its first corporate client, Kodansha. Isidcore is now open to commissions.

  3. 03

    Kodansha House — Los Angeles & New York

    Client work · 2026 · Audio supervision & composition

    Kodansha, Japan's largest manga publisher, ran Kodansha House as a series of pop-up fan experiences. For its Los Angeles and New York (Hudson Yards) editions, Isidcore served as audio supervisor and composer, creating the music and immersive audio for all five activations. Each activation was built around one Kodansha property:

    • Blue Lock
    • AKIRA
    • Attack on Titan
    • Ghost in the Shell
    • Witch Hat Atelier

    Studio roles: Audio supervisor · Composer · Immersive / spatial audio

  4. 04

    Sincerely, Mr. Love & Pop — End-of-Summer Party

    Event · July 29, 2026 · Beverly's, New York

    A pre-release party for the mini-album, co-hosted with Blizzy McGuire and presented with Life After Trance. It ran 5pm to 2am. Isidore had met more than 300 creatives in New York since March, and the night was meant to bring them together in one room.

    DJs: Océane, Robyn Dabank, Cute Boys, Gagbert, Aster, Mini Lis

    Studio roles: Concept · Booking · Promotion · Host

  5. 05

    Isidcore: J-Pop Night @ Kodansha House

    Event · October 15, 2026 · Kodansha House, Hudson Yards

    After the audio commission, Isidcore was invited back to program its own night inside the Kodansha House NYC pop-up. The set list covered J-pop, J-core, and anime openings across eras, from Hikaru Utada and Ayumi Hamasaki to Perfume, m-flo, and Crystal Kay. The night also promoted Sincerely, Mr. Love & Pop.

    DJs: Aster, Bubucore, Cute Boys, Gagbert

    Studio roles: Programming · Booking · Promotion · Host

About

Isidore Lu (isid or) is a musician, visual artist, and model based in New York. He writes, produces, and engineers his own electronic pop, and directs and edits the visuals that go with it. He founded Isidcore to run all of this work, and work for clients, under one studio.

He studies Music & Technology at Carnegie Mellon University.
